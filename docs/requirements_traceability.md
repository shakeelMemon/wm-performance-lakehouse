## Wealth-management domain coverage

* **Canadian context:** registered plans (RRSP, TFSA, RESP, RRIF, FHSA), RRSP/TFSA contribution seasonality, CAD reporting with USD conversion, and a November–October fiscal year.
* **Book of business:** Mass Affluent, HNW and UHNW households. Discretionary, fee-based and commission programs.
* **Advisor hierarchy:** advisor, then branch, then region, with **as-was attribution** when advisors transfer branches (SCD2).
* **Standard WM KPIs:** AUM (semi-additive), NNA, organic growth, market movement, ROA in bps, TWR, NPS, complaint rate, SLA and attrition.
